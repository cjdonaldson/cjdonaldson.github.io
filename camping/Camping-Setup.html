RV Capacities

RV Holding Tanks reference

  • 44 gal fresh
  • 33 gal gray
  • 32 gal black
Usage estimates
  • ~4 gals per person per day
    • dishes → gray
    • showers → gray (navy shower < 2 minutes)
    • toilet → black
    • teeth brushing → black
    • hand washing → black
  • 2 people = 8 gals/day → 3 days = 24 gals
Site Setup
  1. Inspect and clear the site
  2. Position the RV — blocking to get close to level
  3. Set parking brake
  4. Chock the wheels
  5. Shore power
  6. Block and leveling jacks
  7. Slide out
  8. Water
  9. Sewage
Clock
  1. Press and hold the clock button
  2. Enter time in 24-hour format
  3. Press clock button again to save
Auto-level Nulling
  1. Manually level the RV
  2. Power off the leveler
  3. Depress and hold auto-level
  4. Press and release power button
  5. Wait for 5–6 beeps
  6. Release auto-level button — null is now set
Tank Cleaning

The Truth About RV Black & Gray Tanks | How to Prevent Clogs, Odors & Sensor Problems

Fresh water tank
  1. Fill with water
  2. Add 16 oz of bleach
  3. Let sit for 24 hours
  4. Drain and flush with fresh water
Gray water tank
  1. Fill with water
  2. Add 16 oz of Dawn Ultra
  3. Let sit for 24 hours
  4. Drain and flush with fresh water
  5. Perform last night before leaving — dump on exit
Black water tank
  1. Fill with water
  2. Add 1 oz of RV Digest-It+
  3. Let work for 24+ hours
  4. Flush
